Primed artist canvas

Linen-blend base cloth for hand painting, UV / Latex / inkjet printing and stretched-canvas production.

Genuine linen blend

Real flax in the base cloth gives a natural woven character that all-polyester canvas does not have, at a lower cost than pure linen.

Multiple-coat priming

White primer applied in multiple passes. Ready to paint or print straight off the roll.

Direct manufacturer liaison

You deal with the production team for quotes, samples and export support.

Dual purpose

Oil and acrylic hand painting, and roll-to-roll UV, Latex and inkjet printing.

Custom widths

Made to order, 150 cm to 200 cm, with private-label and custom-coating options.

Consistent batches

Uniform coating and weave run after run, so finished pieces stay predictable order after order.

Runs from 5,000 m

Runs are set from 5,000 m of base cloth, which is what keeps the weave and the coating consistent from batch to batch.

Third-party tested

A representative primed linen-canvas sample passed CPSIA total-lead, phthalate and ASTM soluble-heavy-metal testing. Record available on request.

Base cloth composition

45%Polyester
25%Viscose (rayon)
15%Linen (flax)
15%Cotton

Base cloth

A woven linen blend containing flax. It has a natural woven character that all-polyester canvas does not, at a lower cost than pure linen.

It is not pure linen and is not supplied as such. For a 100% linen requirement this is not the correct product.

Labelling

Composition is listed in descending order by weight, as US FTC and EU 1007/2011 textile labelling require. It is the same figure your customs broker and your lab will see.

Priming: multiple coats of white primer. Surface arrives ready to paint or print.
