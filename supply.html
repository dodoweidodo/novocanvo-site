Sampling

Evaluation rolls

For qualified converters we arrange and cover the first evaluation courier: typically one full-width metre each of 4438 and 3388, plus smaller gloss and matte pieces if printing is in scope. Dispatch dates are confirmed once we have the delivery address and a freight quote.

For general enquiries, swatch cards are sent free and the courier is quoted at cost.

Documents

  • Product catalog (PDF) — grades, widths, packing
  • Material-safety test record — single-sample, limits stated
  • Indicative FOB range — once widths and grades are fixed
  • Landed-cost indication — EU / US, against a current tariff and freight quote

Figures are indicative until confirmed in a formal quotation. Prices are quoted per enquiry.

Consistency

Coating weight and weave are checked during each production run. If cloth source or coating formulation changes, we inform you before the next run is booked.

Export desk

One contact, English and Chinese, Singapore time (GMT+8). Replies within one business day. Documents are arranged for your customs broker.
