What we do

Weaving, priming, inspection.

Linen-blend base cloth, woven and primed to specification — multiple coating passes, rolled in the lengths you order.

Runs are set from 5,000 m of base cloth, which is what keeps the weave and the coating consistent from batch to batch.

The cloth is made for stretched-canvas production and for roll-to-roll UV, Latex and inkjet printing.
